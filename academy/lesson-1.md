# The First Impression

### Why the first 30 seconds matter

A guest decides whether they're going to enjoy their evening **before they sit down**. The storefront, the door, the first eye contact — these are not details. They are the foundation.

### The 10-Second Rule

From the moment the door opens, someone must **acknowledge the guest within 10 seconds**. Eye contact. A nod. A smile. A simple "Be right with you" buys you two full minutes of patience.

**What this looks like in practice:**
- Host stand is always staffed, even during slow periods
- If the host steps away, every server knows to glance at the door
- No guest stands at the entrance feeling invisible — ever

### Why It Works

People don't judge a restaurant by the food alone. They judge it by **how they felt** from the moment they walked in. A warm welcome covers a thousand small mistakes later. A cold one makes the best steak taste average.

### The Promise

At Richie's Tavern, **no guest waits more than 10 seconds to be seen**. This is not a goal. This is the standard.
