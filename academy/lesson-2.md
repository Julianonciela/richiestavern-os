# Reading the Room

### The skill that separates good from great

Most servers react. Great servers **anticipate**. The difference is reading the room before anyone says a word.

### What to Look For

| Signal | What It Means | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| Guest looking around with menu closed | Ready to order | Approach within 30 seconds |
| Empty glass pushed to edge of table | Ready for another round | Offer the refill, don't wait to be asked |
| Guest leaning back, plate half-finished | Slowing down | Give space, don't clear yet |
| Two guests leaning in close | Private conversation | Minimal interruption, silent service |
| Guest checking phone repeatedly | Waiting for someone or bored | Acknowledge: "Can I get you something while you wait?" |

### The Golden Rule

**Serve the table at their speed, not yours.** Some tables want the 90-minute experience. Some want to eat and go. Read the pace — don't impose yours.
