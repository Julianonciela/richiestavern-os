# The Service Sequence

### The backbone of every shift

Service isn't a series of random tasks — it's a **choreographed sequence** that repeats at every table, every night. When the sequence breaks, guests notice. When it flows, they don't — and that's the goal.

### Richie's Tavern Service Sequence

| Step | What | Timing | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Welcome** | Eye contact within 10 seconds, seated within 2 minutes | Immediate | Host |
| **2. Water & Menu** | Water poured, menu handed, specials mentioned | Within 60s of seating | Server |
| **3. Order** | Drinks first, then food — suggest, don't upsell | 3-5 min after water | Server |
| **4. Drinks Delivery** | All drinks arrive together | Within 5 min of order | Bar/Server |
| **5. Food Fire** | Kitchen ticket fired at the right moment | Per pace of table | Server |
| **6. Check-Back** | "How is everything?" after 2 bites | 2-3 min after food arrives | Server |
| **7. Clear & Reset** | Plates cleared together when all guests finish | Within 3 min of last fork down | Server/Busser |
| **8. Dessert / Coffee** | Offer, don't push. Read the room. | Right after clearing | Server |
| **9. Check Drop** | Present check, thank the guest by name if possible | When guest signals or slows | Server |
| **10. Goodbye** | Eye contact, "Thank you for coming," door held if possible | As guest leaves | Everyone |

### Why It Matters

A sequence is not a script. It's a **safety net**. When the room is full and you're overwhelmed, you don't have to think about what comes next — the sequence tells you. Trust it.

### In Practice: A 45-Minute Table

| Minute | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-1 | Welcome + Seat |
| 1-3 | Water + Menu + Specials |
| 3-7 | Order taken (drinks + food) |
| 7-12 | Drinks served |
| 12-22 | Food arrives (kitchen fires at minute 8 for a 20-min dish) |
| 22-25 | Check-back |
| 25-28 | Clear plates |
| 28-32 | Dessert/coffee offer |
| 32-40 | Check + payment |
| 40-45 | Goodbye + table reset |
