One Standard

"If you were the guest, how would you want to be treated?"

That's the rule Richie set. Everything that follows answers that question — the same way, every night, whether there are two tables or a full house.

Service is the minimum.
Hospitality is the rest.

Service is getting the right plate to the right table at the right time. Hospitality is the moment the guest feels truly seen. A room can run perfectly and still feel cold.

The systems below free up the team — and Richie first — to focus on that second part. Richie sits with his friends. Everything else runs itself.

Table Turns

Roll-ups prepped in advance

Silverware rolled and stocked on the main station shelf. Turning a table becomes one move: clear, wipe, place a roll-up and a glass. One motion.

Fixed stock per station

Restocked by the busser or host — never by a server mid-service. Bus tubs never stay visible on the floor.

Two people per table

One clears, the other resets. The room never looks cluttered.

The Team — Three Habits

1. Pre-shift briefing

Same time, same length, every service. A ritual, not a chore.

2. A signal for help

Chosen by the team themselves. No chasing a manager mid-rush.

3. Any server, any table

No one should ever hear "I'll go get your server." It's the fastest way to make a guest feel like a number.
